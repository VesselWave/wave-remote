Wave Remote 0.6.4 brings the Wave family branding to the app:

- A white wave inside a blue TV on the home screen and splash screen.
- The updated launcher icon also works on Android 7–7.1.

Installs over 0.6.3 and keeps your pairing and remote settings. To check the download: `sha256sum --check wave-remote-v0.6.4.apk.sha256`.

Setup and troubleshooting are in the [README](https://github.com/VesselWave/wave-remote#readme). Not affiliated with LG.

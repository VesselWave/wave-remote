Wave Remote 0.6.6 keeps the familiar remote layout across screen sizes:

- Volume and channel stay beside the stacked Apps and Keyboard buttons.
- Controls scale to the available screen space, with a full-width Buttons/Touchpad switch.
- Back, Home, and TV settings stay at the bottom; larger text can still scroll.

Installs over 0.6.5 and keeps your TV pairings and remote settings. Uses the same production signing key as the VesselWave F-Droid repository.

To check the download: `sha256sum --check wave-remote-v0.6.6.apk.sha256`.

Setup and troubleshooting are in the [README](https://github.com/VesselWave/wave-remote#readme). Not affiliated with LG.

Wave Remote 0.6.3 gives touchpad scrolling two separate settings:

- The right-edge scroll strip and two-finger scrolling each have their own direction, Traditional or Natural, in Remote settings.
- Both start out Traditional: content moves opposite to your fingers. If you'd chosen Natural before, choose it again.

Installs over 0.6.2 and keeps your pairing. To check the download: `sha256sum --check wave-remote-v0.6.3.apk.sha256`.

Setup and troubleshooting are in the [README](https://github.com/VesselWave/wave-remote#readme). Not affiliated with LG.

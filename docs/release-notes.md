Wave Remote 0.6.8 refines the remote’s press feedback:

- Volume and channel controls use softly tilted oval highlights around their icons.
- Buttons/Touchpad ripples stay within each rounded choice.

Installs over 0.6.7 and keeps your TV pairings and remote settings. Uses the same production signing key as the VesselWave F-Droid repository.

To check the download: `sha256sum --check wave-remote-v0.6.8.apk.sha256`.

Setup and troubleshooting are in the [README](https://github.com/VesselWave/wave-remote#readme). Not affiliated with LG.
